I. GENERAL Section 1
A) Welcome
A1. Overview
  1. Welcome to the Duke Networking Fantasy League (DNFL).
  2. This rulebook describes the DNFL league bylaws, including league structure, league rules, and expectations of league owners.
  3. The rulebook is organized so users can easily navigate to and reference specific rules as necessary.

    For example, Rule II.A1.01 references the rule which defines the league entry fee amount.

A2. Last Update - July 27, 2026
  1. Updating rulebook in 2026 due to league expansion to 36 teams.
  2. The Rule Book below outlines the rules for 2026-27 season which comprises a Super League with 3 conferences of 12 teams each competing in a single bracket to crown the DNFL champion.
  3. For 2027-28 season and beyond we will be moving to a Relegation Structure with 2 Leagues: 1 Premier League of 24 Teams, 1 Relegation League of 12 Teams. This new structure is outlined in the last section of the Rule Book (Rule Section VIII.F: OTHER - Future Relegation Structure).
B) League Management
B1. Commissioners
  1. The DNFL League is managed by a team of three commissioners: Evan Bryman, Rob Fuqua, and Jason Rosario.
  2. Any questions or clarifications regarding rules or regulations should be directed to the three commissioners.
II. ACCOUNTING Section 2

A1. Entry Fees

  3. All entry fees are to be paid before the draft is started.

  5. Administrative fees for hosting the league are approximately $250 per season, bringing the total purse to approximately $8,750.
  6. All prize money will be dispensed via PayPal one week after the Superbowl.

III. LEAGUE Section 3
A) Conference & Division Assignments
A1. Conferences & Divisions
  1. This is a 3 Conference league: Each Conference has 2 Divisions; Each Division has 6 teams.
  2. Every 3 years teams will be re-distributed across the Divisions (this rule was first implemented in 2018).
  3. Upcoming realignment years are: 2027 - 2030 - 2033 - 2035.

    4. Note: There is also an alignment in 2026 due to the expansion to 36 teams.

A2. Team Assignment Rules

    Note: The Team Assignment Rules below define the assignment process for the 2026 season based on the expansion to 36 teams and restructuring to 3 Conferences. These assignment rules will be revisited prior to the 2027 season based on the new Relegation Structure of the league that will be in effect at that time.

  1. The returning 28 teams will be placed across the 3 Conferences based on final standings, using a snake placement approach to distribute teams into the 6 Divisions as evenly as possible.
  2. The commissioners will try to ensure all keepers for returning teams are honored and may adjust team Conference assignment accordingly.
  3. The 8 new expansion teams will be placed randomly across the 6 Divisions.
B) Standings
B1. Standings
  1. Standings are determined for each Division by overall wins vs losses.
  2. If 2 or more teams have the same win/loss records, total points accumulated is used as the tie-breaker.
  3. If 2 or more teams have the same record and same total points, the regular season head-to-head records are used as the tie-breaker.
  4. If a tiebreaker is needed for any situation that is not explicitly stated in the rules, a commissioner from the non-interested Conference will flip a coin to break the tie.
  5. The final standings after 12 weeks of play will determine who competes in the Playoffs.
C) Rosters
C1. Rosters
  1. Each team can carry 16 players.
  2. There is neither an Injured Reserve (IR) bench nor Taxi Squad.
  3. Injured players may be kept on a roster, but will occupy one of the 16 roster spots.
  4. At all times each team must have a complete 16 player roster, containing at least enough players in each position to submit a valid starting lineup.
  5. There are no limits to how many players at each position a team may own.
D) Lineups
D1. Starting Lineups
  1. 1 QB
  2. 2 RB
  3. 2 WR
  4. 1 TE
  5. 1 FLEX (RB/WR/TE)
  6. 1 PK
  7. 1 DEF
  8. TOTAL OF 9 STARTERS
D2. Lineup Rules
  1. A starting lineup that does not consist of the above (too many or too few in any position) will be considered illegal and will result in a forfeit.
  2. Starting a player who is on a bye-week is legal, however the player will score zero points that week.
  3. Starting lineups may be made at any time before a player's game has started.
  4. Players whose games have started will be locked for that week.
  5. If a lineup is not submitted by the deadline, the team's starting lineup from the previous week will be used (In Week 1, not submitting a lineup will result in a forfeit).
E) Schedules
E1. Regular Season
  1. Regular season play will take place during Weeks 1-12 of the NFL season.
  2. Each team will play one head-to-head game each week (each team plays 12 regular season games).
  3. Each team will play each of their 11 Conference opponents once during the regular season, between Weeks 1-6 and 8-12 of the season.
  4. Inter-Conference games will be played in Week 7, matching teams from different Conferences based on their draft order.

    5. Note: Week 7 Inter-Conference matchups may feature games where teams have the same players in their starting lineups.

E2. Post-Season
  1. Post-season play will take place during Weeks 13-16 of the NFL season.
  2. The Championship Bracket will include 16 teams in total, composed of the top performing regular season teams from each the three Conferences.
F) Draft
F1. Draft Format
  1. There will be 16 rounds in the draft.
  2. Pick order will snake - the odd-numbered rounds will be 1 to 12, then 12 to 1 in the even numbered rounds.
  3. Each year the draft will be held on either the third or fourth Saturday of August (final date and time to be determined by the commissioners).
  4. Draft picks may be traded (see Transactions/Trades for more details).
  5. The draft will be a live online draft.
  6. Draft Timer is set to 1 minute 30 seconds - meaning each owner will have 90 seconds to make their pick.
  7. If the Draft Timer expires, the system will automatically pick a player for the owner based on ADP rankings.
  8. Keeper picks will be entered by the commissioner in the appropriate slot on the draft board before the draft begins.
  9. If an owner misses a pick or selects the wrong player, the owner must immediately notify the commissioners.
F2. Draft Order

    Note: The Draft Order below has been updated due to the expansion to 36 teams.

  1. Expansion teams will get the first picks in each conference.
  2. The draft order for returning teams will be based on final standings from last season.
G) Supplemental Draft Picks
G1. Supplemental Picks
  1. Supplemental draft picks are awarded to team owners who accomplish life events.
  2. Teams with multiple owners must select a primary owner for awards.
  3. Life events must occur between August 1st of prior year and July 31st of current year.
  4. Supplemental picks are awarded in exchange of another draft pick of the owner's choice.
G2. Life Events and Supplemental Draft Pick Awards
  1. 10th Round Pick — Rewarded for getting married, having a child, or celebrating a decade birthday.
  2. 8th Round Pick — Rewarded for an owner's personal promotion, new job, IPO/sale, or retirement.
  3. 6th Round Pick — Rewarded for getting a Duke alumnus a new job.
  4. 4th Round Pick — Rewarded for getting any other CCMBA'07 classmate a new job.
  5. 2nd Round Pick — Rewarded for getting a DNFL owner a new job.
  6. Two 1st Round Picks — Rewarded when an owner becomes CEO/CFO of Fortune 500 or Duke Basketball head coach.

V. TRANSACTIONS Section 5
A) Waivers (Add/Drop)
A1. General Waiver Information
  1. All Free Agents (FAs) are locked at the start of the week and are available for Blind Bid (BBID) waiver requests.
  2. BBID requests process Wednesday at 12pm ET, followed by First Come First Serve (FCFS) waivers until Sunday 1pm ET.
  3. Owners must always drop a player to add an FA.
A2. BBID $
  1. Each team begins the season with $100 of BBID money.
  2. Minimum bid is $0; bids increment in $1 steps up to remaining balance.
B) Trades
B1. Trade Deadlines
  1. Week 9 Trade Deadline: Trades involving players drafted in rounds 1-3.
  2. Week 11 Trade Deadline: Standard deadline for all other trades and future draft picks.
B2. Trade Processing & Rules
  1. Trades must be proposed and accepted via the website.
  2. Owners can trade up to $30 in BBID $ per season.
  3. Future draft picks (3rd round or later) can be traded. Equal number of picks must be exchanged.
VI. CHAMPIONSHIPS Section 6
A) Last Team Standing
A1. Last Team Standing Overview
  1. Survivor pool taking place during Weeks 2-12 of the season.
  2. The lowest-scoring team each week in each Conference is eliminated.
B) Playoffs
B1. Championship Bracket Schedule
  1. Week 13 — First Round (16 Teams)
  2. Week 14 — Second Round (8 Teams)
  3. Week 15 — Semifinals (4 Teams)
  4. Week 16 — DNFL Super Bowl Championship
VII. KEEPERS Section 7
A) Overview & Cost
A1. Keeper Eligibility & Draft Forfeiture
  1. Each team can keep up to four players from the previous year.
  2. To keep a player, forfeit a draft pick 2 rounds earlier than drafted.
  3. Player must have been drafted in round 3 or later and never dropped to Free Agency.
VIII. OTHER Section 8
A) Governance & Future Relegation
A1. Rule Changes & Expulsion
  1. Rule changes require a majority vote (19 or more owners).
  2. Failure to submit lineups for 4 consecutive weeks triggers commissioner failure-to-manage clause.
A2. Future Relegation Structure (2027+)
  1. In 2027-28, league splits into Premier League (24 teams) and Bedeviled League (12 teams).
  2. 4 teams promoted / relegated annually between leagues.
