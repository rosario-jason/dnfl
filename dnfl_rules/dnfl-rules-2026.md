# I. GENERAL

## A) Welcome

### A1. Overview
1. Welcome to the Duke Networking Fantasy League (DNFL).
2. This rulebook describes the DNFL league bylaws, including league structure, league rules, and expectations of league owners.
3. The rulebook is organized so users can easily navigate to and reference specific rules as necessary.
> *For example, Rule II.A1.01 references the rule which defines the league entry fee amount.*

### A2. Last Update - July 27, 2026
1. Updating rulebook in 2026 due to league expansion to 36 teams.
2. The Rule Book below outlines the rules for 2026-27 season which comprises a Super League with 3 conferences of 12 teams each competing in a single bracket to crown the DNFL champion.
3. For 2027-28 season and beyond we will be moving to a Relegation Structure with 2 Leagues: 1 Premier League of 24 Teams, 1 Relegation League of 12 Teams.

## B) League Management

### B1. Commissioners
1. The DNFL League is managed by a team of three commissioners: Evan Bryman, Rob Fuqua, and Jason Rosario.
2. Any questions or clarifications regarding rules or regulations should be directed to the three commissioners.

# II. ACCOUNTING

## A) Entry Fees

### A1. Entry Fees
1. The league entry fee is $250 per team.
2. All payments must be paid via PayPal to ebryman@yahoo.com.
3. All entry fees are to be paid before the draft is started.
4. With 36 teams each paying $250, our league starting balance for each season will be $9,000.
